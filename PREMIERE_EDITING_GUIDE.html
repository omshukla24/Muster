🎙️ Audio & Color Grading Secrets

Voiceover Processing (Make Phone Audio Sound Like Studio)
Select Track A1 → Open Essential Sound panel:
1. Assign as Dialogue.
2. Check Loudness → Auto-Match (-23 LUFS).
3. Check Clarity → Dynamics (set slider to 2.5).
4. Check EQ → choose Warm Voice or Subtle Boost.
Sound Effects Placement (Track A2)
• 0:25: Crisp mouse click when pressing "Run Audit".
• 0:55: Deep cinematic bass boom when ghost nodes break.
• 1:12: Quick warning whoosh when flipping to LIVE mode.
• 1:25: Real phone dial tone + operator's real spoken sentence!
Color & Text Sharpness (Lumetri Color)
Add an Adjustment Layer across V1:
• Contrast: +12
• Highlights: +8
• Shadows / Blacks: -6 (gives dark UI deep inky blacks).
• Sharpen: +15 (ensures ledger text and numbers look razor-sharp on 1080p).
Export Settings for YouTube / Devpost
Press Ctrl + M (Export):
• Format: H.264
• Preset: Match Source - Adaptive High Bitrate
• Target Bitrate: 16 - 20 Mbps (VBR 1 pass)
• Audio: AAC 320 kbps, 48 kHz, Stereo.
