✂️ The 5 Rules to NOT Look "Basic"
1. Punch-in Zooms (115% – 130%) Every 4 to 6 Seconds Never let a raw 1920x1080 static screen sit for more than 5 seconds. Whenever you talk about a button, dial, or node, keyframe scale to 125% centered on that element.
2. Fast Speed Ramps on API & Dialing Delays During the 12-call demo audit, speed up the middle 10 seconds to 200% or 300%. The judges want to see the velocity, not wait for progress bars.
3. Sound Design (The Secret Sauce) Add subtle SFX: UI clicks when buttons are pressed, a digital whoosh on transitions, a low sub-bass boom when ghost nodes crack in the Reveal finale, and a telephone dial tone during live call.
4. Background Music Ducking Pick a low-tempo tech/lo-fi beat. Set volume to -24dB to -28dB under your voice, and bump it to -14dB during the 3-second pause before the Reveal!
5. Bold Lower-Third Callouts Use sleek dark rounded pill boxes (e.g. #141822 with oxblood border) for captions like "CALLED AT RUNTIME", "30 PASSING TESTS", "SENATE 80% GHOST RATE".
